\chapter{AI Sandbox Generated Malware Analysis Report}
\label{app:ai-sandbox-report}

This appendix provides the complete AI-generated malware analysis report produced by the SOCaaS AI Sandbox during the controlled laboratory validation scenario discussed in Chapter~4. The report was generated after the suspicious sample was executed inside a temporary isolated VM and analyzed through the sandbox workflow. It includes the reversed logic recovered by the sandbox, extracted indicators of compromise, MITRE ATT\&CK mapping, risk assessment, and recommended analyst response actions.

The appendix is included in full to preserve the evidentiary value of the validation. It demonstrates that the AI Sandbox produced a complete analyst-readable artifact rather than a brief alert summary, thereby supporting first-level malware triage and reducing the amount of manual reverse engineering required during incident response.
